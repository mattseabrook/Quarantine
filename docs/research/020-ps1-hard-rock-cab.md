# PS1 / Hard Rock Cab Notes

PS1 analysis is second phase.

Use jPSXdec or equivalent to inspect the BIN/CUE.

Look for:

- TIM images
- STR video
- XA audio
- CDDA tracks

Do not commit ISO/BIN/CUE or extracted PS1 assets.

PS1 assets may help identify corresponding DOS sprites/textures if the formats are easier to decode.
